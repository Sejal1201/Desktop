Famous Places in India

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

A beautiful white marble monument and symbol of love.

2. India Gate, Delhi

A famous war memorial located in New Delhi.

3. Jaipur

Jaipur is famous for its forts, palaces and pink buildings.

4. Goa

Goa is famous for its beaches and beautiful coastline.

5. Manali

Manali is a popular hill station surrounded by mountains.
